The pictures in this folder are this map's own textures (packed by dms: src/dms/extras.py).

CACOSTAR.png  The star carved on the ordinary obelisks. From the mapper's artwork
              (../art/Gemini_Generated_Image_23fctc23fctc23fc.jpg: made with an image generator from
              Freedoom's MARBFAC2 texture), scaled down to 128 x 128.
CACOFACE.png  The eye in the passage's and the stairs' carved alcoves. Freedoom's GSTSATYR texture with the
              carving of Freedoom's MARBFAC2 scaled down and blended over the satyr's face.

CACOSTAR.png and CACOFACE.png are derived from textures of the Freedoom project, whose licence follows.

CBRUNE.png    The rune on the great door's small door (decaldef.txt's CacobeliskRune). Original, drawn by
              ../art/rune_decal.py; nothing of Freedoom's in it (the repo's MIT licence covers it).
