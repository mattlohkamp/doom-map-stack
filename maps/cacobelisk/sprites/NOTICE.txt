The pictures in this folder are this map's own sprites (packed by dms between SS_START and SS_END:
src/dms/extras.py).

CBDC*.png  The cyan station drone (zscript.zs CacobeliskDrone): idle A-B, staring C-D, firing E, pain F.
CBBC*.png  Its bolt: in flight A-B, popping C-E.
CBDR*.png  The red station drone (CacobeliskRedDrone), the same frames.
CBBL*.png  Its bigger bolt.

Drawn from scratch by ../art/drone_sprites.py (a small ray tracer; re-run it to redraw them). The metal is in
Freedoom's palette; the glowing parts are in true colour. The design follows Freedoom's chaingunner (a red visor
slit in a grey helmet) and revenant (small red lights), but no Freedoom pixels are copied. Their death uses
Freedoom's own rocket explosion (MISL), which stays in the IWAD.

CBDY*.png, the yellow drone (2026-11-26), the same way: drawn by ../art/drone_sprites.py, nothing of Freedoom's.

CBGN*.png, CBGP*.png, the drone gun (2026-11-27; zscript.zs CacobeliskDroneGun): the cyan drone held close and seen
from behind (first person, A-E) and lying powered down (the pickup). Drawn from scratch by ../art/drone_gun.py, which
uses ../art/drone_sprites.py's model; nothing of Freedoom's.
