Send Claude a note

Here: click a spot on a build's picture.

In the editor: select something (or point at it), then Ctrl+Shift+N, or Ctrl+right click, or Tools > Send to Claude.

In the game: N, then type the note.

On the map: red circles are monsters (the line shows where they face), blue diamonds things to pick up, small grey circles lights and decoration, green the player start.

The map: mouse wheel zooms, drag pans, F fits it again. Labels appear as there's room for them: zoom in on a crowded spot. Point at something to read what it is.

Board keys: ← → flip builds, End latest, Space replay.

In the game

H loads Claude's latest build and puts you back where you stood.

Stuck in a wall or outside the map after H (the layout moved under you)? Open the console (~) and type map *: the map starts over from the player start, with a pistol start.
